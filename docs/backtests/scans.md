## Reading

Written 2026-10-23 after the first run; updated after the user's decisions the same day (#136): the VCP floor below is in, the numbers above are with it.

- **It runs clean.** No exceptions over 6 years of the point-in-time universe; 58 s of scanning for ~3M member-sessions, so the scans are cheap enough for the 3.8 backtest to call every session.
- **Counts are plausible for a watchlist, not a trade list.** VCP 16 setups a median session (2,500-6,700 a year; fewest in 2020 and 2022, when fewer stocks passed the Trend Template), momentum 3, the EP proxy 1. The same base appears on consecutive sessions until it breaks out or breaks down. Ranking and the top-40 cap (3.3) and the regime decide what reaches the Analyst.
- **VCP levels look right.** The structural stop is 5.4% below the pivot at the median (2.8-8.6%), inside Minervini's 8% from the limit; the final contraction is 5.4% deep and its volume 0.75 of the 50-day. Most setups have 2 contractions (85%), then 3 (14%). The extremes method finds the big swings only: a rally from the base low that dips on the way up is one leg, not two contractions.
- **Finding 1, VCP: merger-arbitrage flatliners.** 6.5% of VCP setups have a first contraction under 5% (KTWO 1.3% -> 0.7% in the samples); under 4% they are mostly acquisition targets pinned near the deal price (UFS, MANT, NGHC, KS, ABAX, STAY, FGL, EVOP, MENT, NUAN). They pass the Trend Template after the deal jump, and no guardrail stops them (their ATR is tiny too). The Analyst would discard them, but the rule-only baseline book would buy them. Share of setups by first-contraction depth: < 8% 18%, < 10% 29%, < 12% 41%. **Decided (#136): the first contraction must be at least 5% deep.** That removes the flatliners (2,011 setup-days, 5%) but not the 5-10% band, which is mostly real operating companies with shallow bases (MSFT, BA, SNPS, CDW, ADSK, COST); the few deal targets that still trade wider than 5% (ACIA, SWC) are left to the Analyst. The first run, without the floor, had 38,014 setups.
- **Finding 2, momentum: deep consolidations.** The consolidation's pullback is 18.9% at the median, but 31% of setups pull back more than 25% and 20% more than 30% (NKLA 44%). They satisfy "higher lows, tightening range" on halves, but they aren't tight flags, and their pivot sits far above the close (18.8% at the 90th percentile; 11% reach it the next day). The order-time stop (low of the day, at most 1 x ADR) bounds the risk of the few that trigger. **Decided (#136): no cap now;** the 3.8 baseline reports momentum results by consolidation depth.
- **EP proxy:** a 15.4% median gap on 6.1x volume; 54% closed above their open. An EP enters on the gap session itself, so it has no next-day reach; its pivot here is the gap day's open, a floor under the real opening-range high, and the simulator (3.6) decides the fill. The earnings flag isn't wired in this run.
- **Overlap:** 602 sessions where a stock is both a VCP and a momentum setup; both are kept (they have different entries and exits), and 3.3's ranking picks.
