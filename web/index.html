AuraTrade trades US growth stocks the way documented momentum traders do. Claude chooses the setups, sizing and exit plans. Python computes every price level, carries out every plan and enforces hard risk limits the model cannot override.

Who decides what

The split is the core of the design: judgement goes to the model, arithmetic and safety stay in code.

Claude decides

  • Which of the evening's ranked setups are worth taking
  • How much of the risk budget each one gets
  • Which exit plan each position follows, from a fixed menu
  • Its own, more conservative, read of the market regime
  • Whether to tighten an existing plan, never loosen it

Code enforces

  • Every indicator, pattern, base count and price level
  • Share counts, position size and total open risk
  • A protective stop resting at the broker for every share held
  • Each position's exit plan, mechanically
  • Fail closed: on any doubt, no new positions

A trading day

  1. 17:00
    Ingest and regime. Daily bars, macro and earnings data come in; five market-health pillars set the regime.
  2. 17:35
    Scan. Trend Template, then VCP, momentum, pullback, emerging-leader and episodic-pivot scans, ranked and capped.
  3. 17:50
    Analyst. Claude reviews up to 40 candidates overnight in one batch, with charts and fundamentals.
  4. 08:45
    Portfolio Manager. Claude allocates the day's risk across approved setups. No valid decision by 09:10 means no new entries.
  5. 09:36
    Execution. Limit-protected entry orders at the pivot or the opening-range high, each with its stop attached.
  6. 09:35–16:00
    Stop guard. Every 5 minutes, code checks that every position has a full-size stop at the broker.
  7. 15:40
    End-of-day review. Close-based exit rules run; Claude is consulted only if a decision is needed.

Times are US Eastern. Every job first checks the market calendar.

Market regimes

Exposure follows the market, IBD-style. A downgrade tightens open positions but never forces a sale.

RegimeStrategies fundedBase risk / tradeMax exposure
Confirmed uptrendVCP and momentum breakouts, episodic pivots, leader pullbacks1.0%100%
Under pressureHigh-conviction pullbacks, breakouts and episodic pivots0.5%50%
Early uptrendEmerging leaders and episodic pivots, as starter positions0.25–0.5%25–50%
CorrectionNone: cash—Existing only

Base risk is then scaled by a progressive-exposure multiplier: smaller after losses and drawdowns, larger when trading well.

Limits the model can't override

Guardrails are pure functions with full branch coverage. They run on every action the model proposes, and anything outside them is clipped or rejected.

1.5%max equity at risk on any one trade
6%max total open risk across all positions
25%max equity in any single position
2max positions per correlated cluster
−2% / −5%daily / weekly loss halts on new entries
−15%drawdown halt, re-enabled only by hand
  • Price levels come from code. The model may nudge a pivot within ±3% or tighten a stop. It never supplies an unchecked price.
  • Stops always rest at the broker. No halt, kill switch or error path ever cancels a protective stop or blocks a planned exit.
  • Plans only tighten. The exit plan is written at entry, and later decisions can only make it more conservative.

How it's judged

An LLM's decisions can't be honestly backtested, so the evidence is built forward and every decision is logged.

Baseline book

A rule-only portfolio runs alongside on the same signals and fill model. The LLM has to be no worse on expectancy and drawdown.

Verdict scorecard

Every buy and skip verdict is simulated forward under a default plan, so skipped setups are scored too.

Backtest discipline

Rules are tuned on 2016–2022 only, with point-in-time universes including delisted stocks. 2023 onward is kept for confirmation.

Live trading starts only after a paper trial of at least 3 months or 30 trades, whichever is later, passes criteria set in advance.
