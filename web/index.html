An LLM momentum book
on a short leash.

Once a month, AuraTrade ranks the 1,000 most liquid US stocks by their past-year return and holds a handful of the strongest. Claude chooses which of the top-ranked names to hold and when to step aside. Python computes the ranking and every price level, keeps a stop at the broker for every share and enforces hard limits the model cannot override.

  • 12-1momentum: the past year's return, skipping the last month
  • 8equal positions, rebalanced monthly
  • 20%protective stop at the broker on every position
  • <$150monthly running cost, serverless on AWS

Who decides what

The split is the core of the design: judgement goes to the model, arithmetic and safety stay in code.

Claude decides

  • Which of the 16 top-ranked candidates fill the book's empty slots
  • Whether a position gets a full slot or half
  • When to exit early, for example before an earnings report or on bad news
  • Its own, more conservative, read of the market regime
  • Whether to raise a stop, never lower it

Code enforces

  • The ranking, the candidate list and every price level
  • Share counts, slot sizes and the exposure cap
  • A protective stop resting at the broker for every share held
  • The rule exits: a holding that drops out of the top 10% is sold
  • Fail closed: on any doubt, no new positions

A month, and a day

  1. Every evening
    Ingest, regime, ranking. Daily bars, macro and earnings data come in; five market-health pillars set the regime; the universe is ranked by momentum.
  2. Month end
    Book Analyst. Claude reviews the top 40 names and every holding overnight in one batch, with charts, fundamentals and news.
  3. 08:45
    Portfolio Manager. On the first session of the month, Claude picks from the top 16 for the empty slots. No valid decision by noon means no new buys that day.
  4. 15:40
    Rebalance. Holdings that left the top 10% are sold, then the new buys go in as limit orders, each with its stop attached.
  5. 09:35–16:00
    Stop guard. Every 5 minutes, code checks that every position has a full-size stop at the broker.

Times are US Eastern. Every job first checks the market calendar. Between rebalances, only the stops and Claude's early exits act.

Limits the model can't override

Guardrails are pure functions with full branch coverage. They run on every action the model proposes, and anything outside them is clipped or rejected.

16candidates: the only names that can be bought
1/8of equity at most in a new position
100%max exposure: no margin, ever
2max positions per correlated cluster
−2% / −5%daily / weekly loss halts on new buys
−30%drawdown halt on new buys, re-enabled only by hand
  • Price levels come from code. The model may raise a stop. It never supplies an unchecked price.
  • Stops always rest at the broker. No halt, kill switch or error path ever cancels a protective stop or blocks an exit.
  • Plans only tighten. The model can exit earlier or raise a stop, never hold past a rule exit or lower a stop.

How it's judged

The rule book is backtested under rules fixed in advance; the LLM's decisions can't be honestly backtested, so that evidence is built forward and every decision is logged.

Backtest gate

The rule-only book must beat SPY and the MTUM momentum ETF on 2017–2022, after costs, in whole shares at $5,000, then once more on 2023 onward, opened a single time.

Rule book

A rule-only book takes the same candidates in rank order, on the same fill model. The LLM book has to be no worse.

Scorecard

Every name Claude reviews is scored by how it does until the next rebalance, so its convictions are tested, including the names it passed on.

Live trading starts only after a paper trial of at least 6 rebalances or 3 months, whichever is later, passes criteria set in advance.
